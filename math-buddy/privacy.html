Privacy Policy

For Math Buddy: Fun Math for Kids (Android package com.xoigame.mathbuddy) · Last updated: 21 November 2026 · Effective: 21 November 2026

The short version

  • Math Buddy is made for children aged 3 to 11. It collects no personal information from anyone.
  • No ads. No analytics or tracking SDKs. No accounts, no login, no chat, no social features.
  • Progress and settings stay on the device. They are never sent to us.
  • The one-time "Unlock all games" purchase is handled by Google Play or the App Store and sits behind a parent gate.
  • Questions: xoistudio@gmail.com.

1. Who we are

Math Buddy: Fun Math for Kids (the "App") is developed and published by XOI GAME (XOI Studio), based in Vietnam. The App is available in English and Vietnamese.

2. Information we collect

None. The App does not collect, store, transmit or share personal information, device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), location, contacts, photos, audio or any usage data. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or other third-party tracking software development kits, and it has no accounts or login.

The App does not request the camera, microphone, location or contacts permissions.

3. Data stored on the device

To remember progress, the App saves stars, unlocked content, backups of that progress and settings in the App's local storage on the device. This data never leaves the device and is removed when the App is uninstalled.

4. Reminder notifications

The App can show a reminder to practice. It is off by default and can only be turned on by a parent or guardian in the "For parents" area. Reminders are scheduled locally on the device; they are not sent through any server and involve no personal data. They can be turned off in the App or in the device settings at any time.

5. Purchases

The App offers one optional, one-time in-app purchase ("Unlock all games"). It can only be started behind a parent gate (an adult math question). Payment is processed entirely by Google Play or the App Store under their own privacy policies (for Google Play, Google's Privacy Policy); we never see payment or billing details. The App only receives a confirmation from the store that the item is owned, so it can unlock content and restore the purchase.

6. Links outside the App

"Rate us", "More games" and the privacy policy link are only available behind the parent gate and open a web page or the store in the device's browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

7. Children's privacy

The App is designed for children and complies with the Google Play Families Policy, the Apple Kids Category guidelines and the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Because we do not collect personal information from children, no parental consent for data collection is needed. If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example, by emailing us), contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Since we hold no personal data from the App, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. If you email us, we use your email address only to reply and delete the conversation on request.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
