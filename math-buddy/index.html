Math Buddy: Fun Math for Kids

Count. Learn. Grow!

Math Buddy is a gentle math app for children aged 3 to 11, from kindergarten to grade 5. Short, playful games build counting, addition, subtraction, multiplication and more at the child's own pace. There are no ads, no tracking and no accounts, and everything works offline. English and Vietnamese are included.
