Sandy Pop: Color Sand Puzzle

Drop. Flow. Pop!

Drag a colorful block into the glass tray and watch it melt into soft, flowing sand. Link one color from the left wall to the right wall and the whole band flashes and pops. The sand above tumbles down, and if it links up again you set off a chain reaction. Then spend your stars on Pip the hermit crab's beach.
