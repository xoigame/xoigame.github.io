Purr Quilt: Cat Logic Puzzle

Purr. Think. Solve!

Every quilt hides a perfect napping spot for each kitten. Place one cat in every row, every column and every color patch, but cats love their space: they never touch, not even at the corners. Cross out squares with the X tool, follow pure logic to the one true solution, then decorate Mochi's cozy cottage.
