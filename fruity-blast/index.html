Fruity Blast: Fruit Pop Puzzle

Tap. Pop. Blast!

Tap a group of two or more matching fruits and watch them burst. Pop a big group to build a rocket, a bomb or a rainbow, put two boosters side by side for a super combo, and watch boosters set each other off in a chain reaction. Then spend your stars on Buzzy the bee's farm.

300 juicy levels

From a gentle first board to BIG levels every 10 levels. Rockets clear a row, bombs blast everything around them, rainbows pop a whole fruit, and wooden crates and sticky honey keep you thinking.

Help whenever you need it

A HOW TO PLAY page is one tap away, UNDO is always free, HINT shows the best tap and the board shuffles for free when there is no move. No timer, no lives, you can't lose.

03

Build Buzzy's farm

Spend stars to build the Apple Orchard, Berry Patch and Sunflower Hill, unlock six fruit styles in their own baskets and come back every day for the daily puzzle.
