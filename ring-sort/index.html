Ring Sort: Color Hoop Puzzle

Stack. Sort. Satisfy.

A cozy wooden-toy puzzle. Tap a peg to lift the rings of the same color on top, then drop them on another peg. Sort every peg to a single color to lock it with a lid. 300 levels bring mystery rings, frozen pegs, chained pegs, boss puzzles and a daily challenge.
