Hexagram 58: The Joyous Lake (兌 Duì) – I Ching Meaning

Hexagram 58 of the 64 I Ching hexagrams is called The Joyous Lake (兌, Duì). It is made of the lower trigram Dui (Lake) and the upper trigram Dui (Lake). Keywords: joy, sincere conversation, shared pleasure. This is for reflection and entertainment, not a prediction.

Judgment

Real joy is shared and it comes from the heart. Be warm, honest and firm within, and enjoy time with others.

The Image

Two lakes lie side by side. Learn from each other through friendly conversation and shared interests.

The Joyous Lake hexagram meaning

General meaning

A bright, friendly period. Enjoy good company and share your good mood.

Love

Laughter and honest conversation bring you closer. Enjoy lightness, with sincerity under it.

Career

Positive teamwork and open talk help. Keep morale up, and keep promises.

Advice

Enjoy it, and keep it sincere. Joy lasts when it is honest.

The six changing lines

  1. Line 1. A quiet, easy joy from a contented heart. Simple harmony is enough.
  2. Line 2. Honest, sincere joy brings clarity. Doubts fade when you are true.
  3. Line 3. Joy chased from outside brings emptiness. Look for contentment from within.
  4. Line 4. Weighing choices of pleasure takes thought. Choose lasting peace over a quick thrill.
  5. Line 5. Trust where there is some wear and tear. Stay alert to flattery that might erode your judgment.
  6. Line 6. Being pulled by charm and temptation leads you off course. Pause and decide with a clear mind.

In short

Real joy is shared; be warm, honest, and let your smile spread.
