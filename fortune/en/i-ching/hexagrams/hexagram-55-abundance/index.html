Hexagram 55: Abundance (豐 Fēng) – I Ching Meaning

Hexagram 55 of the 64 I Ching hexagrams is called Abundance (豐, Fēng). It is made of the lower trigram Li (Fire) and the upper trigram Zhen (Thunder). Keywords: fullness, clarity, peak. This is for reflection and entertainment, not a prediction.

Judgment

Things are at their fullest, like the sun at noon. Enjoy and share the abundance, and remember that peaks pass.

The Image

Thunder and lightning arrive together. Be decisive and fair, and let your clarity do the work.

Abundance hexagram meaning

General meaning

A high point of energy and results. Enjoy it fully and without fear of what comes after.

Love

Warmth and connection are rich right now. Appreciate it and share it.

Career

Results are strong and visibility is high. Use the moment well and stay level-headed.

Advice

Savour the high point and stay grounded. Use what you have to help others.

The six changing lines

  1. Line 1. Meeting a good match brings strength. Stay a while, since a bit of time together is enriching.
  2. Line 2. The way is dim as if curtains are drawn. Do not push; trust your own steadiness and wait.
  3. Line 3. The light is dim in the shadows, yet you can still do your part. Do not blame yourself.
  4. Line 4. Dimness again, but you meet a like-minded person. Join forces.
  5. Line 5. Bright talents come to you. Be open to them, since praise and good fortune follow.
  6. Line 6. Building too large a house and shutting yourself off gets lonely. Stay connected.

In short

You are at a peak; enjoy it, and save some for lean days.
