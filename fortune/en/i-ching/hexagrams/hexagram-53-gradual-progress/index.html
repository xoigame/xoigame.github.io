Hexagram 53: Gradual Progress (漸 Jiàn) – I Ching Meaning

Hexagram 53 of the 64 I Ching hexagrams is called Gradual Progress (漸, Jiàn). It is made of the lower trigram Gen (Mountain) and the upper trigram Xun (Wind). Keywords: patience, steady steps, proper order. This is for reflection and entertainment, not a prediction.

Judgment

Real progress is slow and gets built one step at a time. Respect the proper order of things and the result will last.

The Image

A tree grows on the mountain. Keep your character steady and improve the surroundings little by little.

Gradual Progress hexagram meaning

General meaning

Slow, steady development works best here. Do not skip steps and do not rush.

Love

A relationship grows gradually, with respect for pace. Courtship and commitment each take time.

Career

Climb through steady milestones. Reliable growth beats quick leaps.

Advice

One step at a time. Trust the slow path.

The six changing lines

  1. Line 1. The first step toward shore is hesitant, with a bit of talk. Stay patient, and it is fine.
  2. Line 2. Settling on a rock in comfort, you share a simple meal. Enjoy what is stable.
  3. Line 3. Going too far, too fast, leaves you exposed. Stay within what is safe.
  4. Line 4. Landing on a branch gives you a place to rest. Find a modest spot that suits you.
  5. Line 5. Progress is held by obstacles for a time, but wins out in the end. Stay patient.
  6. Line 6. Your mission is done and your example guides others. Your steady steps become a model.

In short

Good things grow slowly, so build step by step and do not rush.
