Hexagram 43: Breakthrough (夬 Guài) – I Ching Meaning

Hexagram 43 of the 64 I Ching hexagrams is called Breakthrough (夬, Guài). It is made of the lower trigram Qian (Heaven) and the upper trigram Dui (Lake). Keywords: resolution, candour, decisive speech. This is for reflection and entertainment, not a prediction.

Judgment

One last thing needs to be cleared out. Speak up openly and decisively, but do not turn to force.

The Image

The lake has risen to the sky. Share your resources generously and keep your values front of mind.

Breakthrough hexagram meaning

General meaning

A decision or honest conversation is due. Be clear, direct and calm.

Love

Something unspoken needs air. Say it kindly and plainly.

Career

It is time to resolve a long-lingering issue. Be transparent and firm, without drama.

Advice

Speak the truth with care. Be decisive, not harsh.

The six changing lines

  1. Line 1. Rushing in with too much force can backfire. Be sure you are ready before you act.
  2. Line 2. Staying alert, even in the night, keeps you safe. Be watchful and calm.
  3. Line 3. Showing anger or irritation openly may isolate you. Act decisively but keep your composure.
  4. Line 4. Feeling stuck, you move haltingly. Listen to advice, even if you do not like it.
  5. Line 5. Be firm about letting go of the stubborn habit, and stay centred. Clear resolve does the work.
  6. Line 6. Do not assume the matter is over. Keep watching, since it may not leave on its own.

In short

Speak up clearly and calmly about what must change, then act on it.
