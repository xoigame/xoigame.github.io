Hexagram 30: The Clinging Fire (離 Lí) – I Ching Meaning

Hexagram 30 of the 64 I Ching hexagrams is called The Clinging Fire (離, Lí). It is made of the lower trigram Li (Fire) and the upper trigram Li (Fire). Keywords: clarity, warmth, dependence. This is for reflection and entertainment, not a prediction.

Judgment

Light needs something to burn, and you shine best when you attach to what is steady and good. Gentle, caring energy brings luck.

The Image

Light follows light. Keep your awareness bright and share your warmth with the people around you.

The Clinging Fire hexagram meaning

General meaning

Clarity and warmth are available. Choose what you hold on to carefully, so your light stays steady.

Love

Warm, honest affection shines. Be gentle, and do not cling to the point of burning out.

Career

Insight and visibility are rising. Anchor yourself in good values and reliable people.

Advice

Be bright, but not blinding. Attach yourself to what is good for you.

The six changing lines

  1. Line 1. Footsteps cross in all directions at dawn. Move with focus and respect.
  2. Line 2. Warm golden light, calm and balanced. Stay steady and enjoy it.
  3. Line 3. The sun is setting, so make peace with endings. Enjoy what is here instead of fretting.
  4. Line 4. A sudden blaze burns fast and fades fast. Avoid rushing or flaring up.
  5. Line 5. Tears and sorrow clear the heart. After feeling it, things look brighter.
  6. Line 6. Take clear, firm action to correct what is wrong, but stay measured and kind.

In short

Be bright and clear like a flame, and give it something good to hold.
