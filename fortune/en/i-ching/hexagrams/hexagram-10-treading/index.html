Hexagram 10: Treading (履 Lǚ) – I Ching Meaning

Hexagram 10 of the 64 I Ching hexagrams is called Treading (履, Lǚ). It is made of the lower trigram Dui (Lake) and the upper trigram Qian (Heaven). Keywords: conduct, care, courage. This is for reflection and entertainment, not a prediction.

Judgment

You are walking near something powerful. Stay courteous, calm and aware, and you will pass safely.

The Image

Heaven above, lake below: everyone has a place. Be clear about your role and conduct yourself well.

Treading hexagram meaning

General meaning

The situation involves someone or something stronger than you. Good manners and calm confidence matter.

Love

Respect each other's boundaries. Gentle, respectful words carry you through tense moments.

Career

You may be dealing with a senior person or a high-stakes task. Prepare well and stay polite.

Advice

Step carefully but without fear. Courtesy is your best tool.

The six changing lines

  1. Line 1. Walk your own simple way. Staying plain and sincere keeps you clear of trouble.
  2. Line 2. The road is smooth and you are at peace. Keep your calm and your integrity.
  3. Line 3. Overconfidence can make you step badly. Know your limits and do not overreach.
  4. Line 4. You are close to danger, yet careful steps lead to a good end. Keep your guard up.
  5. Line 5. Decisive steps are needed, yet stay aware of the risk. Courage and caution together.
  6. Line 6. Look back at the path you have walked. Reflection shows you what comes next.

In short

Walk carefully and politely, even near something strong; good manners protect you.
