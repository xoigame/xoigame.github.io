Hexagram 6: Conflict (訟 Sòng) – I Ching Meaning

Hexagram 6 of the 64 I Ching hexagrams is called Conflict (訟, Sòng). It is made of the lower trigram Kan (Water) and the upper trigram Qian (Heaven). Keywords: disagreement, caution, fairness. This is for reflection and entertainment, not a prediction.

Judgment

A dispute is possible, and being right is not always enough. Stay honest, stay careful, and look for a fair middle point.

The Image

Heaven and water move in opposite directions. Think carefully at the beginning so that small clashes do not grow.

Conflict hexagram meaning

General meaning

Tension or disagreement may be in the air. Slow down and keep the issue small.

Love

A quarrel can grow from a small thing. Listen first and aim to understand, not to win.

Career

Be clear about agreements and put them in writing. Seek a fair outcome over a win.

Advice

Choose resolution over victory. Ask a neutral person for a view if needed.

The six changing lines

  1. Line 1. Do not drag the dispute on. A few words are enough; it ends well.
  2. Line 2. You cannot win this one. Step back and return to a quieter footing.
  3. Line 3. Live on what you have earned honestly. Stay steady and avoid new fights.
  4. Line 4. You lose heart for the argument. Return to calm and think of a better way.
  5. Line 5. A fair, wise judge helps you. Seek an honest hearing and accept it.
  6. Line 6. A prize won by force does not last. Real peace is better than a trophy.

In short

Before arguing, ask what you really want; a calm talk beats winning.
