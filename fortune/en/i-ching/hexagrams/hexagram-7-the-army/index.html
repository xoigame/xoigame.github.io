Hexagram 7: The Army (師 Shī) – I Ching Meaning

Hexagram 7 of the 64 I Ching hexagrams is called The Army (師, Shī). It is made of the lower trigram Kan (Water) and the upper trigram Kun (Earth). Keywords: discipline, organization, leadership. This is for reflection and entertainment, not a prediction.

Judgment

A group effort needs order and a trusted lead. With clear purpose and fairness, a united team can do a lot.

The Image

Water gathers in the earth. In the same way, welcome people in and build strength from the many.

The Army hexagram meaning

General meaning

Collective effort and good organization matter right now. A clear plan keeps everyone together.

Love

Treat the relationship like a shared project with fair rules. Respect and teamwork keep it strong.

Career

Strong structure and a steady leader get results. Delegate well and keep morale high.

Advice

Be clear about roles and aims. Lead with fairness, not pressure.

The six changing lines

  1. Line 1. Set order and rules before starting. A clear start prevents later confusion.
  2. Line 2. Stay close to your team and trust them. A leader who is present earns loyalty.
  3. Line 3. Too many hands pulling different ways leads to mess. Decide who leads and who follows.
  4. Line 4. A calm step back is sensible when conditions are poor. Retreat to regroup, not to quit.
  5. Line 5. Act only when there is a real reason, and choose a capable lead. Careful choice of people matters.
  6. Line 6. When the job is done, reward fairly and give responsibility to the trustworthy.

In short

Good order and a trusted leader turn a group into real strength.
