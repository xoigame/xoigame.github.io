Hexagram 31: Influence (咸 Xián) – I Ching Meaning

Hexagram 31 of the 64 I Ching hexagrams is called Influence (咸, Xián). It is made of the lower trigram Gen (Mountain) and the upper trigram Dui (Lake). Keywords: attraction, responsiveness, openness. This is for reflection and entertainment, not a prediction.

Judgment

Mutual attraction and understanding bring people together. Approach with an open, honest and modest heart.

The Image

A lake rests on the mountain. Stay receptive and open to others, and let yourself be moved.

Influence hexagram meaning

General meaning

Connection flows when you are open and sensitive to others. Let it happen without forcing.

Love

There may be a spark, and it grows through genuine openness. Be warm and honest.

Career

Rapport and mutual respect open doors. Listen well and stay approachable.

Advice

Stay open and sincere. Let attraction grow gently, without pushing.

The six changing lines

  1. Line 1. A small stirring, barely a signal. Notice it, and let it be for now.
  2. Line 2. Moving before you are ready tends to stall. Wait a little and stay steady.
  3. Line 3. Acting on every impulse makes you follow whatever moves. Choose your path on purpose.
  4. Line 4. Uncertain hearts send mixed signals. Be clear and true, and the right people will respond.
  5. Line 5. A steady, firm stance, without regrets, keeps your integrity. Hold your center.
  6. Line 6. Talk alone does not create a real connection. Back up your words with sincere action.

In short

Mutual attraction grows when you stay open and listen with your heart.
