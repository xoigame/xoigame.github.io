Numerology Calculator – Find Your Life Path Number

Numerology reads character and tendencies through numbers drawn from your birth date and name. The key figure is your life path number, found by adding the digits of your birth date until a single digit remains (keeping master numbers 11, 22 and 33).

In Fortune Teller, enter your birth date and name to see your life path number plus supporting numbers such as soul urge, expression and personality, each with a short explanation.

How to read the result

The life path number describes the general lessons and spirit of your road. Numbers 1 to 9 link to themes like independence, cooperation, creativity and stability; master numbers are often seen as asking more of you.

Do not treat a number as a fixed mold. Use it as a small mirror to notice your own strengths.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate my life path number?

Add all digits of your birth date, then keep adding until one digit remains. 11, 22 and 33 are usually kept.

Do I need my name?

The life path number needs only the birth date. Your name is used for soul urge and expression numbers.

Can numerology predict the future?

No. It describes tendencies to help you understand yourself and is for reflection and entertainment.

Are people with the same number alike?

Not entirely. Environment, experience and choices matter just as much.
