Free fortune telling you can use anywhere

Fortune Teller is a free reading app built for reflection and fun. Pull a daily tarot card, cast an I Ching hexagram, build a Chinese astrology chart or calculate your life path number, then read plain-language interpretations in English or Vietnamese. Nothing here promises outcomes; the goal is to give you a calm moment and a fresh angle on the question in your head.

Seven ways to read

If you like imagery, start with a free tarot reading and browse our tarot card meanings for all 78 cards, upright and reversed. For long-term patterns, try Zi Wei Dou Shu, the Chinese astrology chart, or BaZi (Four Pillars) to meet your Day Master and the five elements.

Prefer ancient wisdom? Cast an I Ching reading and look up each of the 64 hexagrams in our hexagram meanings guide, or use Plum Blossom numerology to build a hexagram from a number. Our numerology calculator finds your life path number from your birth date, and playing card reading uses an ordinary 52-card deck for quick answers about love and daily life.

How to get more from a reading

Ask open questions such as "What should I pay attention to?" instead of yes or no. Treat the answer as a prompt, then choose one small action for the week. Do not use a reading to decide health, legal or major financial matters; those need a qualified professional.

Frequently asked questions

Is Fortune Teller really free?

You can use the web version free of charge to try the main readings. The mobile app may offer extra options depending on the version.

How accurate is an online tarot reading?

A tarot reading is not a prediction. It is a tool for reflection, so treat it as a prompt to think rather than a guarantee.

Which reading should I try first?

A daily tarot card is the quickest start. If you want something deeper, build a Zi Wei Dou Shu chart or a BaZi chart from your birth data.

What is the difference between tarot and the I Ching?

Tarot uses 78 illustrated cards to reflect on a question. The I Ching uses 64 hexagrams, cast with coins, to suggest how to act in a situation.

Do I need my birth time?

For Zi Wei Dou Shu and BaZi, yes: birth time shapes the chart. Tarot, I Ching and playing cards need only a question.

What does a reversed tarot card mean?

A reversed card usually points to something blocked, excessive or turned inward. It is a gentle nudge to adjust, not a bad omen.

Can I read in Vietnamese?

Yes. The whole app and these guides are available in English and Vietnamese.

Is my data sent anywhere?

The app is designed to run on your device. See the About section in the app for details on data and privacy.
