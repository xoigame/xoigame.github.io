Six of Wands Tarot Card Meaning: Upright & Reversed

Six of Wands (Sáu Gậy) is a number 6 card in the suit of Wands (Minor Arcana), linked with the element of fire and themes of action, passion and ambition. Upright keywords are recognition, success, confidence; reversed keywords are self-doubt, pride, delayed credit. Below is the Six of Wands tarot card meaning for general guidance, love, career and advice. This is for reflection and entertainment, not a prediction.

Six of Wands upright

Upright keywords: recognition, success, confidence

General meaning

Your effort may be seen and appreciated. Letting yourself enjoy the win can recharge you for what comes next.

Love

Feeling proud of each other or of yourself can warm the bond. Share compliments freely.

Career

Recognition or a good result may come your way. Stay gracious and credit those who helped.

Advice

Write down three things you did well recently. Share one win with someone who supports you.

Six of Wands reversed

Reversed keywords: self-doubt, pride, delayed credit

General meaning

Recognition may be late, or you struggle to feel proud. Your worth does not depend on applause.

Love

Comparing or needing validation may cause strain. Offer kindness to yourself first.

Career

Credit may not arrive as hoped. Keep a clear record of your work and speak up politely.

Advice

Judge your progress against your own past, not others. Ask for feedback you can use.

In short

Upright: Your win is real; accept the praise and let it fuel you.

Reversed: Do not chase applause; trust your own sense of what you achieved.
