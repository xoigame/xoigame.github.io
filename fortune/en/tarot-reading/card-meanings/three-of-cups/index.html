Three of Cups Tarot Card Meaning: Upright & Reversed

Three of Cups (Ba Cốc) is a number 3 card in the suit of Cups (Minor Arcana), linked with the element of water and themes of feelings and relationships. Upright keywords are friendship, celebration, community; reversed keywords are overindulgence, gossip, feeling left out. Below is the Three of Cups tarot card meaning for general guidance, love, career and advice. This is for reflection and entertainment, not a prediction.

Three of Cups upright

Upright keywords: friendship, celebration, community

General meaning

Good company and shared joy are highlighted. Gathering with people who lift you up feels nourishing.

Love

Friends and community may play a happy role in your love life. Enjoy the lightness together.

Career

A friendly team and shared success are possible. Celebrate small wins with colleagues.

Advice

Reach out to friends and plan a get-together. Raise a glass to something you have achieved.

Three of Cups reversed

Reversed keywords: overindulgence, gossip, feeling left out

General meaning

Social life may feel crowded or lonely. Choose company that truly feels good.

Love

Gossip or outside opinions could cloud a bond. Keep private things between the two of you.

Career

Office talk may turn unhelpful. Stay out of rumours and focus on the work.

Advice

Spend time with one friend instead of a crowd. Skip conversations that leave you drained.

In short

Upright: Share your happiness with friends; good company lifts the spirits of all.

Reversed: Too much socializing or gossip drains you; make time for real friends.
