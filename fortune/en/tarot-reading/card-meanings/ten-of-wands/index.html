Ten of Wands Tarot Card Meaning: Upright & Reversed

Ten of Wands (Mười Gậy) is a number 10 card in the suit of Wands (Minor Arcana), linked with the element of fire and themes of action, passion and ambition. Upright keywords are heavy load, responsibility, strain; reversed keywords are letting go, delegating, relief. Below is the Ten of Wands tarot card meaning for general guidance, love, career and advice. This is for reflection and entertainment, not a prediction.

Ten of Wands upright

Upright keywords: heavy load, responsibility, strain

General meaning

You may be carrying a lot, perhaps more than your share. It is a good time to see what can be shared or set down.

Love

Responsibilities can crowd out closeness. Make time for the two of you, even a short one.

Career

Workload may be heavy near the finish. Prioritise and ask for help.

Advice

List everything on your plate and cross out or hand off two items. Finish the rest one at a time.

Ten of Wands reversed

Reversed keywords: letting go, delegating, relief

General meaning

A burden is easing, or you are ready to put some of it down. Lightening the load is a wise move.

Love

Letting go of control may bring you closer. Share responsibilities more evenly.

Career

Delegating or dropping a task may improve your results. Focus on what truly counts.

Advice

Say yes to help when it is offered. Plan something light after a long push.

In short

Upright: You are carrying too much; put some weight down or share the load.

Reversed: It is time to drop a burden you never needed to carry.
