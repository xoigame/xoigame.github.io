Five of Cups Tarot Card Meaning: Upright & Reversed

Five of Cups (Năm Cốc) is a number 5 card in the suit of Cups (Minor Arcana), linked with the element of water and themes of feelings and relationships. Upright keywords are regret, grief, what remains; reversed keywords are acceptance, moving on, forgiveness. Below is the Five of Cups tarot card meaning for general guidance, love, career and advice. This is for reflection and entertainment, not a prediction.

Five of Cups upright

Upright keywords: regret, grief, what remains

General meaning

Disappointment may be loud right now. It is okay to feel it, and it helps to notice what is still standing.

Love

A loss or regret may be on your mind. Give your feelings room, then look at what still holds warmth.

Career

A setback may sting. Learn from it and see which options remain.

Advice

Let yourself feel sad for a set time, then do one small useful thing. Name one thing you still have.

Five of Cups reversed

Reversed keywords: acceptance, moving on, forgiveness

General meaning

You are beginning to accept and move forward. Healing tends to come in small steps.

Love

Forgiveness, of others or yourself, may ease the way. Open your heart again at your own pace.

Career

You may be ready to turn from a past disappointment. Redirect energy to a new plan.

Advice

Write a short note of release about what you let go of. Take a step toward something fresh.

In short

Upright: Grieve what was lost, but turn around; there is still something left standing.

Reversed: You are starting to heal; let the sorrow go and look forward.
