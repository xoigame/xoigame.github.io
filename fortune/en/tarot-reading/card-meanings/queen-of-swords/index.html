Queen of Swords Tarot Card Meaning: Upright & Reversed

Queen of Swords (Hoàng Hậu Kiếm) is a court card in the suit of Swords (Minor Arcana), linked with the element of air and themes of thought, words and decisions. Upright keywords are clear judgment, honesty, independence; reversed keywords are coldness, over-criticism, guardedness. Below is the Queen of Swords tarot card meaning for general guidance, love, career and advice. This is for reflection and entertainment, not a prediction.

Queen of Swords upright

Upright keywords: clear judgment, honesty, independence

General meaning

A clear head and a steady voice help you see what matters. You can be honest without being unkind.

Love

Straight talk with warmth builds trust. Be clear about your boundaries and your needs.

Career

Your sharp judgment is valuable now. Give precise feedback and make decisions on facts.

Advice

Say the true thing in a kind way. Trust your own reading of the situation.

Queen of Swords reversed

Reversed keywords: coldness, over-criticism, guardedness

General meaning

A guarded stance may keep people at a distance. Critical thoughts can get louder than kind ones.

Love

Walls may be up to avoid being hurt. Let one small, honest feeling through.

Career

Criticism might be harsher than needed. Aim for useful, not cutting.

Advice

Soften your tone before you share a hard truth. Let someone close see a little of how you feel.

In short

Upright: Be clear and honest; kind words that tell the truth earn respect.

Reversed: Coldness protects you but also isolates you; let some warmth back in.
