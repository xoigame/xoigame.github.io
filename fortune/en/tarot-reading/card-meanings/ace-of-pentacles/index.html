Ace of Pentacles Tarot Card Meaning: Upright & Reversed

Ace of Pentacles (Át Tiền) is the Ace, the seed of the suit in the suit of Pentacles (Minor Arcana), linked with the element of earth and themes of work, money and material life. Upright keywords are new opportunity, seed money, stability; reversed keywords are missed chance, poor planning, shaky start. Below is the Ace of Pentacles tarot card meaning for general guidance, love, career and advice. This is for reflection and entertainment, not a prediction.

Ace of Pentacles upright

Upright keywords: new opportunity, seed money, stability

General meaning

A practical opportunity is being offered, like a seed ready to plant. It is small now, but it can grow with care.

Love

A solid, down-to-earth connection could begin or deepen. Show care through steady, real actions.

Career

A new job, project or source of income may be on the table. Look at the details and say yes if it fits.

Advice

Plant one concrete seed this week, such as a budget or a first draft. Keep it simple and keep it going.

Ace of Pentacles reversed

Reversed keywords: missed chance, poor planning, shaky start

General meaning

An opportunity may be slipping past, or plans lack a solid base. Rushing could waste a good chance.

Love

Practical matters may be creating friction. Talk openly about money, time and expectations.

Career

A deal or offer may need a closer look. Check the numbers before you commit.

Advice

Slow down and fix the foundation first. Ask for a second opinion on any big spending.

In short

Upright: A solid new chance is in your hands; plant it and tend it.

Reversed: A good chance may slip by; get your plans ready and act soon.
