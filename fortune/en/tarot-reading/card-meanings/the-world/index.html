The World Tarot Card Meaning: Upright & Reversed

The World (Thế Giới) is card number 21 of the Major Arcana, the 22 cards that tell the big chapters of the journey. Upright keywords are completion, wholeness, achievement; reversed keywords are loose ends, delay, almost there. Below is the The World tarot card meaning for general guidance, love, career and advice. This is for reflection and entertainment, not a prediction.

The World upright

Upright keywords: completion, wholeness, achievement

General meaning

A cycle is coming to a satisfying close. You can take a moment to appreciate how far you have come.

Love

A sense of wholeness and shared growth may be present. Celebrate what you have built together or within yourself.

Career

A project or stage may reach a worthy finish. Reflect on what you learned and prepare for the next horizon.

Advice

Mark the finish line in some small way, such as a note or a treat. Then pick your next goal calmly.

The World reversed

Reversed keywords: loose ends, delay, almost there

General meaning

You may be close to finishing, but a few loose ends remain. Patience for the final stretch will pay off.

Love

Something unresolved may keep a chapter from closing. Gentle closure can bring peace.

Career

A project may be slowing near the end. List what is left and finish one item at a time.

Advice

Make a short list of the last three tasks. Do the smallest one first.

In short

Upright: A long journey is complete; celebrate how far you have come.

Reversed: You are almost there; finish the last loose ends before moving on.
