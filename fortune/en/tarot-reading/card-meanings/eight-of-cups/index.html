Eight of Cups Tarot Card Meaning: Upright & Reversed

Eight of Cups (Tám Cốc) is a number 8 card in the suit of Cups (Minor Arcana), linked with the element of water and themes of feelings and relationships. Upright keywords are walking away, seeking more, moving on; reversed keywords are fear of leaving, drifting, returning. Below is the Eight of Cups tarot card meaning for general guidance, love, career and advice. This is for reflection and entertainment, not a prediction.

Eight of Cups upright

Upright keywords: walking away, seeking more, moving on

General meaning

Something that once fit may no longer feel enough. Choosing to look for deeper meaning can be a brave move.

Love

You may be rethinking a situation that lacks what you need. Be honest and kind as you decide.

Career

A role or goal may feel empty now. Exploring new directions could bring fulfilment.

Advice

Name what is missing and what you hope to find. Take one step toward it, even a small one.

Eight of Cups reversed

Reversed keywords: fear of leaving, drifting, returning

General meaning

You may be hesitating to leave, or leaving without a clear aim. Take time to be sure of your reasons.

Love

Mixed feelings about staying or going may cause stress. Give yourself space to think clearly.

Career

You may be tempted to quit or may be stuck. Weigh the benefits before deciding.

Advice

Write the reasons to stay and to go. Wait a few days, then see which list feels true.

In short

Upright: Something no longer fulfills you; walk away gently and look for more.

Reversed: You keep going back to what does not feed you; consider moving on.
