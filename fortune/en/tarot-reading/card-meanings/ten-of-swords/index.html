Ten of Swords Tarot Card Meaning: Upright & Reversed

Ten of Swords (Mười Kiếm) is a number 10 card in the suit of Swords (Minor Arcana), linked with the element of air and themes of thought, words and decisions. Upright keywords are an ending, rock bottom, release; reversed keywords are recovery, slow rise, refusing to end. Below is the Ten of Swords tarot card meaning for general guidance, love, career and advice. This is for reflection and entertainment, not a prediction.

Ten of Swords upright

Upright keywords: an ending, rock bottom, release

General meaning

A painful chapter is closing. It may feel dramatic, yet the worst is often when something finally stops hurting.

Love

A pattern or a connection may be ending. Grieve it honestly and keep your sense of worth.

Career

A project or role may be wrapping up in a hard way. Take the lesson and plan the next step.

Advice

Let the ending be an ending. Rest, then begin a small rebuild when you are ready.

Ten of Swords reversed

Reversed keywords: recovery, slow rise, refusing to end

General meaning

You are beginning to rise after a hard stretch. Recovery may be slow, and that is fine.

Love

Healing is under way, though you may be tempted to revisit what is over. Choose gentleness.

Career

The tough period is easing. Rebuild with steady, modest goals.

Advice

Do not cling to what has already finished. Celebrate small signs of recovery.

In short

Upright: It feels like the end, yet the lowest point means things can only rise.

Reversed: You are slowly recovering; do not rush, but you are on the mend.
