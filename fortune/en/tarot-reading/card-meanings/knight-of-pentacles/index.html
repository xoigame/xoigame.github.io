Knight of Pentacles Tarot Card Meaning: Upright & Reversed

Knight of Pentacles (Hiệp Sĩ Tiền) is a court card in the suit of Pentacles (Minor Arcana), linked with the element of earth and themes of work, money and material life. Upright keywords are reliability, hard work, steady progress; reversed keywords are stagnation, rigid routine, perfectionism. Below is the Knight of Pentacles tarot card meaning for general guidance, love, career and advice. This is for reflection and entertainment, not a prediction.

Knight of Pentacles upright

Upright keywords: reliability, hard work, steady progress

General meaning

Slow and steady wins here. Dependable effort carries you to the finish.

Love

Loyalty and reliability speak louder than words. Show up when you say you will.

Career

Methodical work and careful checking pay off. Stick to the plan, even when it feels slow.

Advice

Keep your promises, including small ones. Do the next task on the list, then the next.

Knight of Pentacles reversed

Reversed keywords: stagnation, rigid routine, perfectionism

General meaning

Routine may have turned into a rut, or caution has become stalling. Something needs to shift.

Love

Predictability might feel dull to one of you. Try a small shared change.

Career

Too much checking can delay results. Set a point where you stop and deliver.

Advice

Break one habit just to see what happens. Finish something that is almost done.

In short

Upright: Slow and steady wins; keep showing up and do the work well.

Reversed: Being too cautious keeps you stuck; try one small new approach.
