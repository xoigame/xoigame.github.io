Free Tarot Reading Online – Draw Your Daily Tarot Card

Tarot is a 78-card deck: 22 Major Arcana cards that map big life themes and 56 Minor Arcana cards in four suits (Wands, Cups, Swords and Pentacles) that cover everyday matters. Each card carries its own imagery and two moods, upright and reversed.

With Fortune Teller you can get a free tarot reading in your browser. Draw a daily tarot card for a gentle theme of the day, or lay out several cards to look at a question from the angles of love, career and advice.

How it works: step by step

  1. Take a breath and think of an open question, such as "What should I focus on in love this week?"
  2. Choose a single card (daily tarot) or a spread for past, present and direction.
  3. Flip the card, look at the image first, then read the upright or reversed meaning.
  4. Write down how it feels and pick one small action.

How to read the result

An upright card shows its energy flowing naturally. A reversed card often points to something blocked, excessive or inward; it is a signal to adjust, not a bad omen.

Each card is explained in four parts: general meaning, love, career and advice. Pick the part closest to your question. To look up any card, see the full guide to tarot card meanings.

Frequently asked questions

Is a free tarot reading accurate?

Tarot is not a prediction. It is a way to look at a question from several sides, so use it for reflection and entertainment.

How often should I draw a daily tarot card?

Once a day, or once per question. Re-drawing until you like the result takes away its value.

What do reversed tarot cards mean?

Reversed cards usually show blocked, excessive or internal energy. Each reversed meaning in the guide includes advice for adjusting.

Can tarot say anything about love?

Each card has a love reading that can help you think about a relationship calmly. Treat it as a conversation starter, not a verdict.

Do I need to memorize all 78 cards?

No. Every card in the app has keywords and an explanation ready to read.
