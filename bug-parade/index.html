Bug Parade: Garden Spray

Hold. Spray. Flutter!

A wiggly parade of bugs is marching along the garden path toward your flower. Help Poppy the little gardener: hold the SPRAY button and your water gun aims all by itself. Every bug you splash turns into a happy butterfly. Level up during the run, pick 1 of 3 power-up cards and stop the parade!

Hold. Spray. Flutter!

01

One-thumb spraying

Hold SPRAY and the water gun aims at the bug closest to the flower. Big clear numbers show how much water each bug needs, great for kids and grown-ups alike.

02

Build your sprayer

Pick POWER, RAPID FIRE, SPREAD, PIERCE, BOUNCE or FREEZE cards to counter shell beetles, speedy ants and splitting grubs, then beat a giant centipede every 5 levels.

03

Grow your garden

Upgrade your water gun between runs, collect water styles from Bubblegum to Rainbow and decorate the Flower Bed, Lily Pond and Greenhouse with your stars.
