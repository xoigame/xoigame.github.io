How it plays

A turn-based stickman archery game

Stickman Archer Duel: Bow War is a turn-based bow and arrow game for Android. On your turn, drag back to aim and release to loose an arrow, then wait while your rival answers. Wind pushes every shot off line, so reading it is half the fight, and a clean headshot ends a duel fast. Hits send stickmen tumbling in ragdoll physics.

Fight through 40 levels across 8 civilizations, each with its own backdrop and a champion waiting at the end. Beat a champion to learn their special arrow, then use it in later duels. Between fights, upgrade your bow and armor and unlock new helmets and looks. When the story is done, keep your aim sharp in Endless mode, the Daily Challenge or the Weekly Tournament. Duels are short, controls need one finger, and the whole game plays offline.
