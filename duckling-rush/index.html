Duckling Rush: Gate Crowd

Aim. Multiply. Rush!

Sneaky raccoons stole Mama Duck's eggs and hid them in their muddy fortress. Drag to aim the egg cannon, send your ducklings through green gates (+5, x2, x3!) to grow the flock, dodge the red ones, bump the raccoon gangs off the bridge and knock the fortress down. Level up during the run and pick 1 of 3 power-up cards!

One-thumb aiming

The cannon fires by itself, just drag left and right. Big clear numbers on every gate and every flock, great for kids and grown-ups alike.

02

Build your duckling army

Pick BIG BATCH, QUICK CANNON, TWIN CANNON, GOLDEN GATES, GIANT DUCK or BRAVE DUCKS cards, then beat a raccoon chief every 5 levels.

03

Grow Mama Duck's pond

Upgrade your cannon between runs, dress your ducklings in cute hats and decorate the Lily Pond, Mill Pond and Forest Creek with your stars.
