Tile Express: Mosaic Train

Tap. Ride. Reveal!

A mosaic picture waits on the board and toy trains ride the track around it. Tap a wagon to send it on a lap: on every line it grabs the first tile it sees if the color matches. Wagons with tiles left over wait in the yard, so plan your order, clear every tile and reveal the picture, then decorate Pip's little station.
