Number Hunt: Find the Number

Someone hid the numbers all over town. Join the little detective, find them in order and crack each case. A calm brain puzzle that trains your focus.

How to play

Tap the numbers in order, starting from 1. Every case hides a new board, and the clock gets a little tighter as you go.

Modes

Story chapters with a new mystery and harder boards.

Daily

One fresh puzzle every day to keep your streak.

Album

Collect stickers and fill the detective's album.
