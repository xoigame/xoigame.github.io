Star Dial: Rotate Ring Puzzle

Spin. Align. Shine!

Every ring around the star has one gap. Tap the right side of a ring to turn it clockwise, the left side to turn it back, or drag it. When the outer ring's gap reaches the star marker at the top, it slides free. Line up the inner rings first for a sparkling combo, then decorate the Stargazer Loft with Ollie, the little star owl.
