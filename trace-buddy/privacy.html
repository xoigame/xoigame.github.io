Privacy Policy

For Trace Buddy: ABC & 123 Tracing (Android package com.xoistudio.tracebuddy) · Last updated: 20 November 2026 · Effective: 20 November 2026

The short version

  • Trace Buddy is made for young children. It collects no personal information from anyone.
  • No ads. No analytics or tracking SDKs. No accounts, no login, no chat, no social features.
  • Progress (stars and stickers) and settings stay on the device. They are never sent to us.
  • The one-time "Unlock everything" purchase is handled by Google Play and sits behind a parent gate.
  • Questions: xoistudio@gmail.com.

1. Who we are

Trace Buddy: ABC & 123 Tracing (the "App") is developed and published by XOI GAME (XOI Studio), based in Vietnam.

2. Information we collect

None. The App does not collect, store, transmit or share personal information, device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), location, contacts, photos, audio or any usage data. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software development kits.

The App does not request the camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage permissions.

3. Data stored on the device

To remember progress, the App saves stars, earned stickers and settings (sound, voice, language) in the App's local storage on the device. This data never leaves the device and is removed when the App is uninstalled. A grown-up can also erase it at any time with "Reset progress" in the For grown-ups area.

4. Purchases

The App offers one optional, one-time in-app purchase that unlocks all letters and numbers. It can only be started from the For grown-ups area, behind a parent gate (an adult math question). Payment is processed entirely by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy; we never see payment or billing details. The App only receives a confirmation from Google Play that the item is owned, so it can unlock content and restore the purchase.

5. Links outside the App

"More games" and "Rate us" are only available behind the parent gate and open a web page or the Google Play Store in the device's browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

6. Children's privacy

The App is designed for children and complies with the Google Play Families Policy and the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Because we do not collect personal information from children, no parental consent for data collection is needed. If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example, by emailing us), contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

Since we hold no personal data from the App, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. If you email us, we use your email address only to reply and delete the conversation on request.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
