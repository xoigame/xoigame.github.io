Trace Buddy: ABC & 123 Tracing

Trace. Learn. Shine!

Trace Buddy helps children aged 3–6 learn to write capital letters, small letters and numbers the right way. Follow the green dot and the arrow, and the app gently checks where each stroke starts, which way it goes and the stroke order — right on the device. Every finished letter earns stars, a cheer from Buddy the owl and a cute sticker.
