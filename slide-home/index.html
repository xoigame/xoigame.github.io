Slide Home: Color Block Puzzle

Slide. Match. Home!

Colorful wooden toy blocks fill a cozy tray, and each color has its own door on the edge. Drag a block anywhere it can slide; when it reaches the door of the same color, it rolls right out. Plan the order, clear the way and send every block home, then decorate Bun the bunny's playroom.

300 relaxing puzzles

From tiny 4x4 trays to big 8x9 boards with rocks and track blocks. Every level is checked to be solvable, and new ideas arrive chapter by chapter.

Help whenever you need it

A HOW TO PLAY page is one tap away, UNDO and RETRY are always free and HINT plays the next move for you. Every color also has a shape, so color-blind players can match too. No timer, no lives.

03

Decorate Bun's home

Spend stars to furnish the playroom, backyard and treehouse, unlock toy styles like Candy and Ocean, and come back every day for the daily puzzle.
